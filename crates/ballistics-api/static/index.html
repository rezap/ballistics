Shot vs. vitals

The vital zone is positioned by eye against each drawing, and its size is a typical figure rather than a measurement. Drag it on the illustration to reposition it, or edit its dimensions above. In hold-over mode the crosshair is draggable too: it marks where the rifle is pointed, not where the bullet lands. Drag it above the vitals and let go — the shot is then solved from that aim point, and the impact moves to where the bullet would actually go.
